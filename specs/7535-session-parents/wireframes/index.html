Session parents: wireframes

#7537, a spike of epic #7535. The data model (approved 2026-11-27) is in ../spec.md. A session can have one parent: a live session on the same host with the label lead. A director is a lead whose children include leads. Each frame shows a Mac (the MVP, #7539) or the Remote and the web page (the port, #7540). All of it is behind the flag session-parents: with it off, no frame here shows, and the tree follows the starter as today.

All names are scratch data. The words follow ASD-STE100. The colours, radii and type are the tokens of design/tokens.json.

1. Choose a lead when you start a session

The Mac starts a session from the prompt bar of a new chat, with chips for the runtime and the worktree. A new chip, Lead, opens a list of the live sessions on this host that have the label lead. The default is no lead: the session goes where it goes today.

2. The nested sidebar and moves by drag

The tree puts a session under its parent when it has one, else under its starter (#835). A lead shows its label as today and gets no new mark. A drag of a row onto a lead sets or changes its parent; a drag to the top of the folder takes it out from under its starter. The row moves at once, and the daemon can refuse (5 below).

3. A move that is refused

The Mac refuses what its cache can see before the drop: the pointer shows the refusal and why, and a drop there does nothing. The daemon can still refuse a drop that the cache let through (an old cache, a lead that lost its label); then the row goes back and a toast says why.
